# IDEAS — shortlist for the next authoring batch

Editor's cut of 60 pitches (two judges, max 10 each). Frame: 184x31, black, notch above x=92.
Rules held: no text, original characters, starts and ends empty, 2-12 s.
Merges: the three giraffe pitches + Dry Spot became one giraffe (gesture) with two follow-ups in the cast section;
the two whale pitches became one (Iona & Bo). Beetle appears once in the batch (Boot Shelter); Domino beetle is benched.

## 1. Recommended first batch (12)

Ranked. 6 gag / 4 gesture / 2 idle. Every clip uses the notch or an edge as a physical thing and has a turn in the last second.

### 1. Fishing From Above — gag — 9/10 (timing-8)
Premise: a hook baited with a worm dangles from the notch; a duck steals the bait; the fisher re-baits with a doughnut.
Beats:
- 0.0-1.8 line drops from the notch with hook + wriggling worm; bobs; one jig.
- 1.8-3.6 duck waddles in from right, walks round the worm (fg/bg swap), looks to camera.
- 3.6-4.8 duck reaches; line yanks up 4 px just before contact; duck freezes; line lowers; repeat twice, faster.
- 4.8-6.0 duck snaps the worm off sideways in one frame, swallows (bulge travels down), waddles off right, smug.
- 6.0-7.2 empty line twitches twice, reels up into the notch fast.
- 7.2-8.6 line comes back with a bigger hook and a doughnut. Hold 0.6. Duck's head re-enters from the right edge, eyes on the doughnut.
- 8.6-10.0 both freeze one beat; line jerks up with the duck's beak clamped on, legs kicking, out through the notch. Empty.
Twist: winner becomes loser in two seconds; the duck goes up into the notch.
Build: duck (2-frame waddle, reach, swallow bulge x3, kick legs), 1-px line, worm (2 wiggle), doughnut ring 5 px. Hoist = fast vertical offset. Stretched to 10 s from the pitch's 9: the ending needed air (director note).

### 2. Egg Drop — gag — 9/10 (notch-object-8)
Premise: the notch is a birdhouse and things keep falling out of it.
Beats:
- 0-2 egg drops from the notch, bounces (squash), wobbles still; crack line; chick pops out, looks up, beak opens.
- 2-4 mother's head pokes down out of the notch upside down, eyes narrowed, grabs the chick by the scruff, hauls it up. Retracts.
- 4-5.5 second egg drops immediately. Head pokes down, stares at the egg, stares at the viewer.
- 5.5-8 egg cracks; a tiny green crocodile climbs out, looks up at her, grins.
- 8-9.5 head withdraws very slowly (ease-in). Croc shrugs to camera, waddles off right.
Twist: the second egg isn't hers.
Build: egg (whole, cracked, halves), chick + croc 12 px two frames each, mother = upside-down head with 2 eye states + scruff-carry chick variant. ~9.5 s.

### 3. Tether (Juno & Bip) — gag — 9/10 (duos-8)
Premise: an astronaut tethers herself to her rover and floats up into the notch; the rover tugs her back and finds out what is on the other end.
Beats:
- 0-2 Bip (14 px rover, whip antenna, one lens) rolls in from left; Juno (20 px astronaut) bounds beside him in 1 s arcs peaking 6 px.
- 2-4 at centre Juno clips a 1-px tether to Bip's antenna, pushes off, floats up into the notch. Tether hangs notch-to-Bip.
- 4-6.5 Bip waits. Antenna wiggles. Lens turns to camera. Tether slackens, then tugs Bip 2 px left, 2 px right; he braces, wheels dig 1 px.
- 6.5-8.5 Bip reverses hard (wheel blur), tether taut; Juno drops out of the notch upside down, dangling 2 px above the floor, helmet dot spinning. Beat.
- 8.5-10 tether yanks the other way: Bip is pulled up into the notch wheels spinning; Juno, still clipped, hauled up feet-first after him. Empty.
Twist: whatever is up there pulls the rover in, and the astronaut follows.
Build: two sprites, tether = pixel line between two anchors, upside-down Juno = vertical flip, 2-frame wheel spin + blur frame. ~10 s. Recurring pair (see cast).

### 4. Boot Shelter — gag — 9/10 (edges-scale-3)
Premise: a beetle nearly stomped twice by a boot from the top edge discovers the notch is the one place the boot cannot reach.
Beats:
- 0-1.5 beetle (12 px) strolls in from left.
- 1.5-2.5 dark-grey shadow ellipse grows under it; it looks up; boot sole slams down from the top edge just behind it. Beetle pops 3 px, lands, sprints.
- 2.5-4 boot lifts. Second shadow ahead; second boot lands just in front. Beetle skids, reverses.
- 4-5.5 beetle dives under the notch. Boot descends over centre and is STOPPED by the notch: bonk (whole strip jolts 1 px), presses twice, gives up, lifts away.
- 5.5-7 beetle steps to the edge of the notch's shadow, looks up, looks to camera, leans back on the notch wall, one leg crossed.
- 7-7.5 one crumb falls from the notch onto its head. Flinch, holds the pose anyway. Cut.
Twist: the notch is the safe zone for once; the smugness gets one crumb.
Build: beetle (walk 2, jump, lean), boot sole one sprite on a vertical rail, shadow ellipse scaled, one crumb pixel, global y jolt. Trim the sole to ~40 px wide (pitch said 60; director flagged crowding). ~7.5 s.

### 5. Giraffe (merged) — gesture: stretch — 9/10 (absurdist-5 + warm-gestures-9)
Premise: a giraffe too tall for the strip walks in headless; its head comes down out of the notch, chewing, does neck rolls, and gets left behind when the body leaves.
Beats:
- 0-2.5 body + legs walk in from left, neck cut off by the top edge; 4-frame walk, gentle bob, tail flick.
- 2.5-3.5 stops with the neck under the notch. Tail swish. 1-px sparkle at the notch lip (found something up there).
- 3.5-4.5 head slides down out of the notch: eyes closed, jaw chewing, content. Opens eyes on the viewer.
- 4.5-7.5 slow tilt left, hold; tilt right, hold; one slow loop down-around-up (offset path, no rotation). Still chewing.
- 7.5-8.5 blink to camera, head slides back up into the notch.
- 8.5-10.5 body walks off right. Head is still visible peeking from the notch; realises; zips off right after the body. Empty.
Twist: the head is left behind in the notch.
Build: body+legs (walk 4f), neck column, head (chew 2f, tilt L, tilt R, blink, look-to-camera). Head motion is offsets with easing. Body must park at exactly x=92 for the neck column to read. ~10.5 s. Recurring character (see cast).

### 6. Black Hole Burp — gag — 8/10 (notch-object-6)
Premise: the notch swallows anything that passes under it, until it meets an anvil.
Beats:
- 0-3 teacup slides in from left, reaches centre, stretches tall (2x tall, half wide), pulled up into the notch. Boot follows, ringed mini-planet, then a fish that wriggles on the way up.
- 3-5.5 anvil slides in, stops centred. Stretches one pixel. Doesn't lift. Dust specks on the strip shiver as the notch strains.
- 5.5-7 anvil finally shoots up. Silence, one beat.
- 7-9 everything erupts back out at once on separate parabolas: fish, planet, boot, teacup pile up; teacup lands upright on top, unbroken.
- 9-10 anvil drops out last, flattens the pile; the teacup pops out sideways off the right edge.
Twist: the notch can't digest the anvil, brings everything back up, and the anvil finishes the job.
Build: 5 object sprites + one stretched variant each, anvil static, 1-frame squash on landing, dust particles. ~10 s.

### 7. The Notch Drips (Camel) — gesture: drink water — 8/10 (warm-gestures-1)
Premise: a camel with two flat humps sips drops from the notch; the humps pop back up one per sip.
Beats:
- 0-1.5 empty; a drop swells on the notch's bottom edge at x=92 (3-4 px, 2-frame grow).
- 1.5-4 camel plods in from left, humps drawn flat as pancakes, head low. 4-frame walk.
- 4-5.5 stops under the notch, tilts head up, catches the drop on its tongue, gulp (throat bob). Hump one pops: flat -> squash -> round with overshoot.
- 5.5-7 second drop forms, second sip, hump two pops. Camel stands tall.
- 7-8.5 turns head square to viewer, blinks once, slow nod. Walks off right, tail swinging.
- 8.5-10 empty. One more drop forms, falls, 3-px splash on the floor. Fade.
Twist: the notch drips once more onto the empty strip; that one was for you.
Build: camel body, walk 4f, head (up/forward/camera), hump prop 3 states, drop 3 grow frames, splash 2f. Draw the flat humps as distinct dark pads so flat vs round reads at 22 px (director note). ~10 s.

### 8. Trunk Call — gag — 8/10 (edges-scale-2)
Premise: an elephant's trunk enters from the left, checks the camera, sneezes, and the empty strip shakes from footsteps you never see.
Beats:
- 0-2 trunk tip creeps in low from the left edge, sniffing (1 px tip wiggle).
- 2-5 trunk extends segment by segment, lifting, until the tip is under the notch. Curls up and taps the notch underside twice.
- 5-6.5 tip flares (wide-nostril frame). Trunk pulls back 4 px in three growing stutters.
- 6.5-7 sneeze: chain snaps straight, 6-8 particles fire up into the notch, trunk whips out the left edge in two frames.
- 7-8.5 silence. The whole strip jolts 1 px up/down three times, slowing. Particles settle.
Twist: the shaking strip after the sneeze is the punchline: it tells you the size of what you never saw.
Build: 5 chained segments; draw 3 hand-tilted frames per segment rather than translating blocks, or the curve goes blobby (director note). Tip 2 frames. Strip shake = global y offset on every layer. ~8.5 s.

### 9. Hibernation — gesture: go to sleep — 8/10 (notch-object-2)
Premise: the notch is a cave and the bear is going to bed in it, blanket and all.
Beats:
- 0-2 bear (22 px) shuffles in from left dragging a blanket so long its tail is still off the left edge. Stops under the notch, yawns (head swap), rubs eye.
- 2-4.5 reaches up, hauls itself into the notch (rise + ease-out). Blanket lies across the strip.
- 4.5-7 a paw reaches down and yanks; blanket slides up into the notch in three tugs, tail last. One corner is left hanging out, rising and falling 1 px (breathing).
- 7-9.5 one slipper drops out of the notch, then the other. Paw comes down, feels around, can't reach; the corner stops breathing (he's awake).
- 9.5-11 paw grabs both slippers, corner tucks up. Empty.
Twist: the slippers wake him, which is what will happen to you if you don't go now.
Build: bear walk 4f, yawn head swap, rise = clip into notch. Blanket = one long tinted rect with 2-frame fringe, moved by offsets. Corner breathing = 1-px oscillation. Paw 6 px on easing. ~11 s.

### 10. Iona & Bo (merged) — idle — 8/10 (duos-1 + edges-scale-4)
Premise: a tiny fish waits at the notch for a whale whose head is longer than the strip; her tail turns out to be further away than that.
Beats:
- 0-2 Bo (10 px orange fish, one big eye) swims in from left on a 1-px sine bob, stops at x~70 facing right. Two bubbles rise from him into the notch.
- 2-5 from the right edge Iona's nose slides in filling the full strip height, then the mouth line, then her 9-px eye at x~150. Nose parks under the notch.
- 5-7 slow 3-frame blink; pupil slides to the viewer and holds. Bo swims up and bumps the nose (1-px kiss). Her eye crinkles.
- 7-9 Bo settles on top of the nose. Iona backs out right at the same slow speed, Bo riding, looking straight at the camera.
- 9-10.5 as the eye clears the right edge, a fluke slides in from the LEFT edge, waves once, slides out. Empty.
Twist: her tail enters from the opposite edge; she is wrapped round the whole machine.
Build: whale = one large static slab moved horizontally only; layered eye with 3 blink frames + 2 pupil positions; 2 bubble particles; fluke = second small sprite. Colour: the flank needs a real blue step off black or it reads as a smudge; that tint is the whole clip. ~10.5 s. Recurring pair (see cast).

### 11. Under the Hat — idle — 8/10 (notch-object-7)
Premise: the notch is the brim of a hat on a head far too big for the strip; all we ever see is its eyes.
Beats:
- 0-2 two 12-px eyes fade open in the dark, one either side of the notch. Long blink.
- 2-4.5 look left, look right, roll up toward the notch (the hat), then straight at the viewer.
- 4.5-7 a 2-px fly zigzags in from the right; eyes track it; it lands on the bridge directly under the notch; eyes cross.
- 7-9 a huge fingertip enters from the right edge (tip only), flicks; fly ricochets off the left edge. Finger withdraws.
- 9-10 eyes close. Reopen once for a last look at the viewer, then close.
Twist: the face never reveals itself; the only other thing that moves is a finger at absurd scale.
Build: eyes with 5 pupil positions + closed/half lids (crossed = pupils inward). Fly 2-frame flicker on a jittered path. Finger = one big sprite sliding from the edge. Cheapest clip here. ~10 s.

### 12. The Clock Clocks Out — gesture: you did enough today — 8/10 (warm-gestures-4)
Premise: an alarm clock about to ring pins its own hammer, yanks its own plug out of the right edge, and lies down with a bell for a pillow.
Beats:
- 0-2 clock (18 px, stubby legs) waddles in from left, cord dragging off the right edge. Minute hand ticks. Hammer twitches.
- 2-3.5 bells jitter 1 px, eyes wide. It pins the hammer with one hand. Jitter stops.
- 3.5-5 grabs the cord, plants feet, tugs twice (lean-back frames); plug pops in from the right edge and flies loose with a spark.
- 5-6.5 hands droop to 6:30 with a wobble. Eyes soften. Looks at viewer, taps its own face twice.
- 6.5-8.5 tips sideways onto one bell as a pillow (3-frame swap); the other bell rings one muffled note (3-px dust puff). Eyes close.
- 8.5-9.5 fade to black.
Twist: unplugged, it rings anyway, once, muffled, then gives up.
Build: body, face with 4 hand states, eyes, bells jitter, hammer, legs walk 4f, 1-px cord, plug, spark. Many small parts at 18 px; keep the bells and hands chunky. ~9.5 s. Director docked it as a stock icon; kept because the self-unplug is the clearest one-idea gesture in the set.

## 2. Recurring cast

### Juno & Bip (astronaut + rover) — from Tether
Why: a loyal-dog rover and a low-gravity astronaut give every clip a physics rule and a heart; the notch is their hatch.
- Sample: Bip drills the floor (dust spray), brings up one crumb; Juno bags it with ceremony, both salute it; the crumb rolls off the edge; Bip chases it, Juno chases Bip. Empty.
- Flat: Bip gets a flat wheel; Juno jacks him up and swaps it; the old wheel rolls off the right edge, and a beat later rolls back in from the left, still going; Bip watches it lap the machine twice, then rolls after it.

### Iona & Bo (whale + fish) — from Iona & Bo
Why: the smallest visitor and the largest; the big one always comes to the small one. Iona's eye can peek in at an edge of any later clip for free.
- Bubble Ring: Iona's mouth line at the right edge blows a bubble ring; Bo swims through it; the ring rises into the notch; Bo follows it up; drops back out a moment later, slightly stunned, and the ring floats out of the notch after him.
- Spout: only Iona's blowhole is visible at the bottom edge; a spout goes straight up into the notch; drops rain back down across the whole strip; Bo opens a leaf umbrella; the notch drips one last fat drop directly onto it.

### The Giraffe — from Giraffe (merged)
Why: a body that never fits is the format's own joke; the head living in the notch is a running gag. Two follow-ups are already pitched.
- Limbo (timing-2): walks in headless, clangs its unseen head on the notch (impact stars, legs splay), head slides down dazed, limbos the neck horizontally under the notch in 3-px steps, springs back upright with overshoot, dips down once more to stick its tongue out at the notch.
- Dry Spot (notch-object-10, trimmed to cat + giraffe): rain everywhere except under the notch; a cat with a leaf umbrella takes the dry patch; the giraffe arrives, parks its neck up into the notch, and everyone is now under a giraffe; rain stops, giraffe leaves, one fat drip from the notch onto the cat.

Also worth a second clip later: the duck (Fishing), the beetle (Boot Shelter + Missing Domino share it), the mother bird (Egg Drop).

## 4. Cut and why

- Giraffe Limbo (9), Giraffe Stretch (9): same lead as the batch giraffe; Limbo kept as a cast follow-up, Stretch's chewing beat folded into the batch clip.
- Passing Whale (8): merged into Iona & Bo (blink under the notch kept, remora dropped for the fluke).
- Dry Spot (7): four animals plus giraffe plus rain in 31 px is a pile-up; trimmed to cat + giraffe as a cast follow-up.
- Ladder to the Notch (7): 11 s of queueing; the ladder-teases-the-third-guy beat is stock.
- Lighthouse Yawns (7): boat + sailor at 12 px is mush; lamp-goes-out is a drift, not a turn.
- Crumb Abduction (7): UFO-beams-tiny-thing is a well-worn desktop gag; the turn is mild.
- Embarrassed Moon (7): blushing kawaii moon idiom; no turn beyond the star copying it.
- The Door Outside (7): ants pointing at the door reads as an instruction; the brief wants nudges through action.
- One for You (Nimbus & Cap) (7): a 1x2 px drop leaving the bottom edge will not read as "for you"; water is covered by the camel.
- Claw Machine (6): known Dynamic Island gag; four plushies plus a claw is dense; the missed grab is the obvious joke.
- Cuckoo Stretch (6): a demonstrated stretch routine aimed at the viewer is an exercise video; giraffe does stretch with a joke.
- Portal Swap (6): the colour-swap payoff needs a second look at 14-18 px; fox-chases-chicken is vaudeville stock.
