Tiny animated visitors
for the MacBook notch.

A free menu-bar app. Every few minutes a character drops by the notch and goes about its business. Click the notch whenever you want another one. Silent, offline, and out of your way.

The black shape is the app. It sits over the hardware notch and opens only while something is happening.

Install

Three steps. The second one is there because the app isn't notarized with Apple, which costs a developer subscription this free project doesn't have.

  1. Unzip the download and drag Ledge into your Applications folder.
  2. The first time only: right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again in the dialog. macOS remembers after that.
  3. Look up. A small 🐌 in the menu bar holds Play a clip, Launch at Login and Quit.

What it is, and isn't

OfflineNo network access at all. Nothing is collected, nothing is sent. There is no account.
LightAbout two hundred lines of AppKit and a folder of animation frames. It idles at zero.
QuietA visitor every few minutes, never a sound. The notch is closed the rest of the time.
Honest about the notchOn Macs without one, the app draws its own at the top of the screen.
