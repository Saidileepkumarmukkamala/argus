### 12. The Clock Clocks Out — gesture: you did enough today — 8/10 (warm-gestures-4)
Premise: an alarm clock about to ring pins its own hammer, yanks its own plug out of the right edge, and lies down with a bell for a pillow.
Beats:
- 0-2 clock (18 px, stubby legs) waddles in from left, cord dragging off the right edge. Minute hand ticks. Hammer twitches.
- 2-3.5 bells jitter 1 px, eyes wide. It pins the hammer with one hand. Jitter stops.
- 3.5-5 grabs the cord, plants feet, tugs twice (lean-back frames); plug pops in from the right edge and flies loose with a spark.
- 5-6.5 hands droop to 6:30 with a wobble. Eyes soften. Looks at viewer, taps its own face twice.
- 6.5-8.5 tips sideways onto one bell as a pillow (3-frame swap); the other bell rings one muffled note (3-px dust puff). Eyes close.
- 8.5-9.5 fade to black.
Twist: unplugged, it rings anyway, once, muffled, then gives up.
Build: body, face with 4 hand states, eyes, bells jitter, hammer, legs walk 4f, 1-px cord, plug, spark. Many small parts at 18 px; keep the bells and hands chunky. ~9.5 s. Director docked it as a stock icon; kept because the self-unplug is the clearest one-idea gesture in the set.
