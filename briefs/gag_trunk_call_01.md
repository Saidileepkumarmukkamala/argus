### 8. Trunk Call — gag — 8/10 (edges-scale-2)
Premise: an elephant's trunk enters from the left, checks the camera, sneezes, and the empty strip shakes from footsteps you never see.
Beats:
- 0-2 trunk tip creeps in low from the left edge, sniffing (1 px tip wiggle).
- 2-5 trunk extends segment by segment, lifting, until the tip is under the notch. Curls up and taps the notch underside twice.
- 5-6.5 tip flares (wide-nostril frame). Trunk pulls back 4 px in three growing stutters.
- 6.5-7 sneeze: chain snaps straight, 6-8 particles fire up into the notch, trunk whips out the left edge in two frames.
- 7-8.5 silence. The whole strip jolts 1 px up/down three times, slowing. Particles settle.
Twist: the shaking strip after the sneeze is the punchline: it tells you the size of what you never saw.
Build: 5 chained segments; draw 3 hand-tilted frames per segment rather than translating blocks, or the curve goes blobby (director note). Tip 2 frames. Strip shake = global y offset on every layer. ~8.5 s.
