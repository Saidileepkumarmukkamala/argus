### 5. Giraffe (merged) — gesture: stretch — 9/10 (absurdist-5 + warm-gestures-9)
Premise: a giraffe too tall for the strip walks in headless; its head comes down out of the notch, chewing, does neck rolls, and gets left behind when the body leaves.
Beats:
- 0-2.5 body + legs walk in from left, neck cut off by the top edge; 4-frame walk, gentle bob, tail flick.
- 2.5-3.5 stops with the neck under the notch. Tail swish. 1-px sparkle at the notch lip (found something up there).
- 3.5-4.5 head slides down out of the notch: eyes closed, jaw chewing, content. Opens eyes on the viewer.
- 4.5-7.5 slow tilt left, hold; tilt right, hold; one slow loop down-around-up (offset path, no rotation). Still chewing.
- 7.5-8.5 blink to camera, head slides back up into the notch.
- 8.5-10.5 body walks off right. Head is still visible peeking from the notch; realises; zips off right after the body. Empty.
Twist: the head is left behind in the notch.
Build: body+legs (walk 4f), neck column, head (chew 2f, tilt L, tilt R, blink, look-to-camera). Head motion is offsets with easing. Body must park at exactly x=92 for the neck column to read. ~10.5 s. Recurring character (see cast).
