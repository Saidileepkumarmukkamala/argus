### 9. Hibernation — gesture: go to sleep — 8/10 (notch-object-2)
Premise: the notch is a cave and the bear is going to bed in it, blanket and all.
Beats:
- 0-2 bear (22 px) shuffles in from left dragging a blanket so long its tail is still off the left edge. Stops under the notch, yawns (head swap), rubs eye.
- 2-4.5 reaches up, hauls itself into the notch (rise + ease-out). Blanket lies across the strip.
- 4.5-7 a paw reaches down and yanks; blanket slides up into the notch in three tugs, tail last. One corner is left hanging out, rising and falling 1 px (breathing).
- 7-9.5 one slipper drops out of the notch, then the other. Paw comes down, feels around, can't reach; the corner stops breathing (he's awake).
- 9.5-11 paw grabs both slippers, corner tucks up. Empty.
Twist: the slippers wake him, which is what will happen to you if you don't go now.
Build: bear walk 4f, yawn head swap, rise = clip into notch. Blanket = one long tinted rect with 2-frame fringe, moved by offsets. Corner breathing = 1-px oscillation. Paw 6 px on easing. ~11 s.
